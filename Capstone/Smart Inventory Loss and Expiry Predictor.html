Problem & Motivation

Why retailers need a smarter way to manage stock

Roughly one third of food produced is lost or wasted worldwide (FAO).

~1/3

Food waste

Retail faces the same issue at a smaller scale: overstock expires, understock loses sales.

Expiry

Spoiled stock

Perishable batches expire before they sell, causing direct money loss.

Loss

Hidden shrinkage

Theft, damage and counting errors go unnoticed until it is too late.

The need: one intelligent system that predicts demand, expiry risk and stock loss together.
Objectives & Scope

What the system will and will not do

Clear boundaries for the project.

🎯 Objectives

  • Forecast product demand accurately
  • Predict which batches will expire unsold
  • Detect unexplained inventory loss
  • Recommend actions: reorder, discount, donate, investigate
  • Reduce waste, stock-outs and financial loss

✅ In scope

  • Retail / grocery stores with batch-wise stock
  • Three AI modules plus a simple dashboard
  • Evaluation on historical and simulated data

🚫 Out of scope

  • Live hardware integration (sensors, RFID)
  • Supplier negotiation and pricing strategy
  • Real-time billing system replacement
